Capabilities:

Auro Pumps provides specialized pumping solutions for critical and high-temperature industrial applications. Its product range includes molten salt pumps and complete molten salt systems or loops, molten zinc and galvalume pumps, molten lead pumps, molten aluminium pumps, vertical submerged pumps, non-metallic vertical pumps, slurry pumps, cantilever pumps, snout pumps, and magnetic drive pumps. The company develops and manufactures specialized pump solutions in-house at its facility in Gujarat, India.

Auro Pumps' molten salt solutions are designed for applications in chemical manufacturing, solar energy, nuclear power, metal refining, glass manufacturing, and other high-temperature industries. Its molten metal pumping solutions are engineered for demanding environments involving extreme temperatures, abrasion, erosion, and corrosion.

The company's vertical submerged and non-metallic pump solutions are suitable for chemical, solvent, corrosive fluid, molten salt, hot oil, slurry, and other challenging applications. Its magnetic drive pumps provide leak-free pumping for applications including chemicals, molten salt, molten metal, and hot oil transfer.

Company Information:

Auro Pumps Pvt. Ltd. was established in 1984 through technology transfer from POMPE VERGANI SpA of Italy. The company specializes in critical application pumping and has developed a broad range of pump series for specific industrial fluids and challenging operating conditions. Auro Pumps serves customers in India and international markets with engineered pumping solutions.

Key Product Applications:

Auro Pumps develops pumping solutions for molten salt circulation, molten metal transfer, furnace circulation, galvanizing lines, chemical processing, slurry handling, corrosive fluid transfer, thermal energy storage, and other critical industrial applications. Its molten aluminium pumps are designed for furnace circulation, emergency pump-outs, furnace maintenance, and transfer between furnaces and ladles.
